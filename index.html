Approach

Every signal is the product of a structured pipeline, not a single prompt. Here is how the system is designed.

  • Multi-agent workflows Specialized agents for detection, analysis, and delivery. Each step is scoped and auditable.
  • Retrieval + citations Every claim traces back to a data source. You can see where the information came from.
  • Guardrails Rate limits, sanity checks, and human override at every critical juncture.
  • Continuous evaluation False positive tracking and model performance reviews. The system is designed to get better over time.
